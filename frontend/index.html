How it works

A Sybil attack is when one adversary creates many fake accounts to inflate or deflate a number's reputation.

Most spam-detection systems count reports naively. 5 fake reports = 5 votes.

SignalTrust's cluster diversity multiplier defeats this: 5 reports from one cluster = 1× weight. Reports from 3 independent clusters = 1.5× weight.

Privacy
Phone numbers are never stored.
Every number is SHA-256 hashed before it reaches the database. The display shows only a masked form (e.g. XXXXX-00001). Even if the DB were leaked, no phone number could be recovered.
Rate-limiting
One confirm per user per report.
The /confirm endpoint rejects duplicate confirmations and self-confirmations, closing the reputation-farming loop.
